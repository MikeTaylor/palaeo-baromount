# Submission to _Palaeontologia Electronica_

Salient points extracted from [_PE_'s contributor instructions](https://palaeo-electronica.org/content/contributor-instructions)


## Initial submission

Omit author names

Title and keywords get a page to themselves

Add plain language summary after abstract. [Five recent plain language summaries](examples-plain-language-summaries) had word-counts of 66, 134, 218, 153, and 246 for a total of 817 and an average of 163 words.

We must suggest the best figure to use for the thumbnail for the paper: 230 x 91 pixels!

Authors submitting new manuscripts should submit a single PDF file that contains all elements of the manuscript, including text, tables, figures, and captions, except for author listing, author affiliations and acknowledgements.

Submitted filenames must be 8+3 monocase!


## Final submission

ABSTRACT heading in all caps

Abstract down to 250 words

A comma should follow "i.e." and “e.g."

In Figure and Table captions, the title "FIGURE 1" or whatever must be in bold capitals

For non-OA images, use: "This image is not published under the terms of the CC-BY licence of this article. For permission to reuse, please see Author et al. (2016)."

Headings:
* First Order are all capitals, bold, centered;
* Second Order are flush left, bold type, regular case
* Third Order are bold, regular case, followed by a period and space, with text on the same line

Dates are in the stupid format "January 1, 1995". But "Either American or British English is acceptable, although manuscripts should be consistent throughout" so maybe ignore that.

In citations, there is a comma between the author and the date (e.g., Smith, 1973).

Unpublished and in preparation citations are not acceptable; they should appear only as personal communications. I will argue this, or post and cite a premature preprint.

Personal communication: (Smith, personal commun., 1995)

Citation of a quotation: (Smith, 1973, p. 16)

Citation of a figure or table: (Smith, 1973, figure/table 3)

Reference format is dumb, and will be postponed

DOIs must be provided in the form https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx -- also stupid

Do not italicize titles of articles, books, or journals.

Acknowledgements are only needed on final submission, and should occur in their own separate page. Initials are used rather than given names but that is stupid.

This manuscript file should consist of the following subdivisions, each starting on a new page:
1. Title page with keywords
2. Abstract and plain language summary
3. Manuscript text
4. References
5. Captions
6. Figures
7. Tables
8. Appendices
